# Artha-Anvaya (अर्थ-अन्वय)

### Meaning Beyond Languages.

**Artha-Anvaya** is an open-source linguistic intelligence initiative focused on helping AI coding agents build accurate, context-aware, and culturally respectful multilingual applications.

It aims to bridge the gap between translating words and truly understanding their meaning.

## The Meaning Behind the Name

The name **Artha-Anvaya** draws inspiration from two Sanskrit words:

### अर्थ — Artha
**Meaning · Purpose · Significance**

*Artha* represents the meaning and purpose behind words and expressions. Language is not merely a collection of words; every expression carries an intention, context, and significance.

### अन्वय — Anvaya
**Connection · Relationship · Contextual Coherence**

*Anvaya* represents the connection and relationship between words, ideas, and their surrounding context. It emphasizes how individual expressions come together to communicate a complete and coherent meaning.

### Artha + Anvaya

Together, these ideas inspire the philosophy of **Artha-Anvaya: preserving the meaning, context, and intent behind language.**

Because multilingual development should be about more than converting words from one language to another. It should preserve what those words are meant to communicate.

## Our Vision

AI coding agents are becoming increasingly capable of building applications, but multilingual development still presents challenges such as:

- Incorrect spelling and grammar.
- Unnatural sentence formation.
- Translations that lose the original meaning.
- Inaccurate cultural and contextual expressions.
- Incorrect pronunciation and transliteration.
- Inappropriate typography, fonts, and script rendering.
- Inconsistent terminology across an application.

Artha-Anvaya aims to address these challenges by providing language-specific knowledge, reusable agent skills, and linguistic validation workflows.

## What We Aim to Build

- **Linguistic Accuracy:** Improve spelling, grammar, and sentence formation.
- **Contextual Translation:** Preserve intent and meaning rather than translating words literally.
- **Cultural Awareness:** Encourage respectful and contextually appropriate language.
- **Pronunciation & Transliteration:** Support accurate language representation and pronunciation guidance.
- **Typography Intelligence:** Help select suitable fonts and ensure correct script rendering.
- **Multilingual Quality Auditing:** Identify linguistic inconsistencies and localization issues in existing applications.

Our goal is to support both the generation of multilingual applications and the auditing of applications that already exist.

## Our Philosophy

**Correct words. Clear meaning. Respectful expression.**

We believe every language deserves accuracy, every culture deserves respect, and every expression deserves to retain its intended meaning.
